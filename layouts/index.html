About

Each year, Team Australia represents Australia as a guest country at the European Cybersecurity Challenge (ECSC).

Team Australia is a team of 8-10 cybersecurity students and young professionals from across Australia.

Our players are selected from the Team Oceania talent pool as funding allows, and additional self-funded players are invited if funding falls short.
