About

Team Australia is a team of 8-10 cybersecurity students and young professionals from across Australia.

The team has represented Australia on the global stage at the European Cybersecurity Challenge (ECSC) each year since first being invited in 2024.

Team Australia participants are selected from the Team Oceania talent pool as funding allows, and additional self-funded players are invited if funding falls short.

Our performance

Team Australia's best overall result at ECSC was 13th out of 37 teams in 2024. Medals for guest teams are handed out based off their placing on the guest team scoreboard.
